How we define incremental sales

We establish a baseline using available sales history before promotion begins. Campaign links, retailer attribution data, referral information and identifiable institutional orders help determine which sales can reasonably be credited to the work. Sales that cannot be reliably attributed are not claimed. We agree the definition and the tracking method before any work starts.

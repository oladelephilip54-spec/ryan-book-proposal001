02 · Phase one

Four workstreams to reach strangers.

A

Book SEO & discoverability

Search-term research around first-time managers and leadership. Clearer book and author pages. Links that point to the right purchase options. Foundational work, not an overnight channel.

B

Influencer & reviewer outreach

Personalised outreach to at least 10 relevant creators. Real responses reported, unanswered messages never counted as results. Paid features need your approval first.

C

Shareable creative

Graphics from the five frameworks and 15 chapters, new-manager questions, common first-time mistakes, and kits for reviewers who feature the book.

D

Cross-platform book trailer

A short trailer for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube. Written and paced for a serious management book. Direction approved before final.

05 · First 30 days

Build, test, then decide.

  1. 1

    Prepare

    Review book pages, find search terms, set the sales baseline, build the landing page and first assets.

  2. 2

    Reach out

    Begin influencer outreach, launch content, verify links and tracking.

  3. 3

    Read signals

    Log responses, follow up, start institutional prospecting.

  4. 4

    Decide

    Keep what works, drop what doesn't, optionally run a small retargeting test.

06 · Success & kickoff

Judged by sales and conversations, not follower counts.

What success looks like

  • Additional sales and attributable earnings
  • Influencer features and real reader engagement
  • Traffic reaching purchase pages
  • Cost per attributable paid sale
  • Qualified institutional conversations
  • Bulk orders and speaking enquiries

Needed from Ryan

  • Existing book links and promo materials
  • Sales history for the baseline
  • Any ad or attribution data you can share
  • Review-copy availability for creators
  • Bulk-purchase terms
  • Any publisher restrictions on ads or links

The opportunity

You already proved people will buy the book. You sold roughly 212 copies in the first three and a half weeks through your own network, without a serious promotional campaign behind it.

That is a useful starting point. The next step is reaching people outside that network who have the same need for what the book teaches.

Who the book is for

Individual readers

New managers, supervisors and team leaders who would rather learn from a practical framework than figure everything out alone.

Book influencers

Creators on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn who regularly feature leadership, management and workplace culture books.

Organizations

Chambers of commerce, municipalities and corporate learning teams. One bulk order of 50 to 100 copies can outperform many individual sales.

Speaking opportunities

The book can help position you for speaking about management and workplace performance.

What the $500 covers

One month of focused work that includes influencer outreach, creative assets and a book trailer ready for every major platform.

Book influencer outreach

Identify and contact at least 10 relevant creators. Personalised messages only. Track responses and any sales impact. Any paid placement needs your approval first.

Creative and artwork for the month

Promotional graphics built around the five frameworks, chapter insights, and questions new managers face. Ready for Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.

Book trailer for all platforms

A short promotional video delivered in formats ready for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube. Direction approved by you before final delivery.

How incremental sales are defined

We establish a baseline using available sales history before promotion begins. Campaign links, retailer data and identifiable institutional orders help determine which sales can reasonably be credited to the work. Sales that cannot be reliably attributed are not claimed. We agree the definition and tracking method before any work starts.

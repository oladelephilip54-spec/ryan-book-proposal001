02 — Who the book is for

Four clear paths to readers

01

Individual Readers

New managers, supervisors, team leaders and department heads who would rather learn from a practical framework than figure everything out the hard way.

02

Book Influencers

Creators on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn who regularly feature leadership, management, personal development and workplace culture books.

03

Organizations

Chambers of commerce, municipalities, corporate learning teams and leadership programmes. One bulk order of 50–100 copies can outperform dozens of individual sales.

04

Speaking Opportunities

The book positions you for speaking engagements about management, leadership and workplace performance — without promising invitations.

03 — The First Phase

Visibility beyond your own network

Four focused workstreams designed to open new channels while we measure what actually produces sales.

A

Book SEO & Discoverability

Make the book easier to find when someone searches for practical management guidance or your name.

  • → Research high-intent terms around first-time managers and leadership development
  • → Improve titles, descriptions and structure on existing pages
  • → Identify relevant articles, interviews and link opportunities
  • → Ensure every asset points to the correct purchase path
B

Book Influencers & Reviewers

Introduce the book to audiences you have not already built. Minimum 10 relevant creators in the first phase.

  • → Leadership, management, business, personal development, workplace culture
  • → Personalised outreach only — no generic blasts
  • → Track responses, features and actual sales impact
  • → Any paid placement requires your explicit approval
C

Creative Content People Can Share

More than a cover image and an Amazon link. Assets built around the ideas in the book.

  • → Short lessons from the five frameworks
  • → Quotes and insights from the 15 chapters
  • → Questions new managers actually face
  • → Platform-native versions, not one-size-fits-all
D

Book Trailer — $50

A short, professional promotional video that introduces the book and directs viewers to purchase. Prepared for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube. Direction approved by you before final render. Built to feel appropriate for a serious management book — not generic AI visuals.

04 — The Sales System

Know what happens after someone sees the book

Dedicated Landing Page

Clean, mobile-first page that introduces the book, explains who it is for, and directs visitors to purchase options. The central destination for all traffic.

Trackable Links

Amazon Attribution and tagged links where supported. Compare channels instead of relying only on impressions and likes.

Retargeting Test

Small Meta retargeting campaign once sufficient traffic exists. Kept separate so we can see cost and evidence clearly.

Clear Reporting

Activity, placements and attributed sales are tracked separately. Ten outreach messages are activity. A verified purchase is a sale.

06 — Partnership Model

Compensation tied to results

You mentioned that if the book sells 1,000 copies in a month, you would be comfortable paying someone $2,500. That is a sensible starting point because it ties compensation to commercial value.

Option A — Performance Partnership (Recommended)

You pay the initial $500 (+ optional trailer and landing page). After setup, my performance compensation is 30% of the agreed net author earnings from incremental sales that can reasonably be attributed to the campaign.
